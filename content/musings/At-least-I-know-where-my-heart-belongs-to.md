What do you fantasize when the first thought of planning a vacation comes to your mind ? Let me narrow it down and ask this question only to people who prefer vacations close to nature. I guess most of the people would think of an exotic resort or hotel by the sea or a cottage inside a jungle or a homestay on some hill, right ? 

That's my guess and I could be wrong, but I can speak for myself. I fancy about those things whenever a vacation comes to my mind. So what's the point I am trying to make ? 

Nothing, I simply want to share what I feel when I plan such vacations and what thoughts dominate my mind when my vacation starts and when I enter the place. The vacation is about relaxation of mind and body, but does it sometime give anxiety or a feeling of emptiness or longing for something ? In my case, the answer is - yes. 

Why ? Like most of you, to me the vacation is about trying to have some experiences, a break away from routine, which relaxes mind and body. One might argue that it's about exploring new places, which is true but the same again boils down to relaxation of mind and body. Now here is my perspective of comparing the same feelings and thoughts when I visit a place or resort or home which I built with love. A place which I call with whatever name, but gives me a feeling that I built this with love and care and its mine. 

## I belong to this place

No matter how luxuries, exotic and cozy stay I get on a resort or a hotel, and no matter how varieties of delicacies they serve, I always know this is going to lasts only for two nights and three days. The humming of these thoughts never go away and that's when I feel - I don't belong to this place.

I admire but don't get attached to the decorations, views, experiences and luxuries. I know somewhere down in my heart that it has a sense of emptiness. For example, I get a view from the room or balcony I always dreamed of having one for myself or my family, but couldn't do it, so I enjoy the view with some judgements, regrets or future plans and that becomes my source of emptiness at that very moment. 

Let's take another example. I see people admiring the crockeries and I do observe the way food is being served on equally tempting crockeries, people immediately start planning to get similar one for themselves or criticize if those crockeries and buffet decorations do not get well with their standards or tastes in either case the feeling remains the same - I don't belong in here. 

Now let me talk about the place which I built and decorated according to my taste. A small home resembling of jungle resort or a small villa or you can relate it with whatever you have built according to your taste. It could also be your home or rented space but not something where you suffocate or not the place which gives you pain in any way. My feelings of vacationing on that place is what surprises me, and often tell me something about me which I wasn't aware of before. The moment I enter that place, I immediately feel arrived. I feel calmer, quieter, static and at peace with surroundings whether or not it's perfect or meet certain standards. I always know where to drop my bags. The happiness when I look at my the bookshelf lined up with books which I had handpicked, some half done, some I have read and some classics I proudly own and proudly boast about. The easy chairs I refurbished few months back, once used by my father. My mom't old radio and the lamps and lightings perfectly in order to welcome me. Trees which I had planted and now grownup like a teenage kids and people who were waiting for me and the joy of hugging them tight and of course the meal which I enjoy with them, well prepared with loads of love, simple, served on old crockeries and steel plates yet delicious and nostalgic. The humming of these thoughts never go away and that's when I feel - I belong to this place. 

I know that the following morning when I will be having my morning tea beneath lemon tree, having hundreds of ripened and equally shallow ripened lemons hanging near to my hand's reach, my mom will be cleaning, chopping and preparing veggies for the lunch with a sense of responsibility and urgency on her facial expressions, In that moment my world will stop as if the universe is observing how I keep looking to my mom. The sight to behold forever, every morning until I breath my last. I believe the blessings also arrive when you look at your parents with love. This is where I belong. 

I know in my backyard garden my brother will disturb my quite reading time by giving me braking news of the day or mocking my attire, or my look or anything he finds funny and then my pre-noon tea with some snacks will arrive. It's all about one things - This is where I belong. 

I know in the same afternoon, just before the lunch, my wife will be drying her hairs after bath under the shadow and warmth of neem tree on backyard garden, often changing the place to get maximum sun, looking at me with immense love and we both won't the saying anything just living in the moment, and finally she will break the silence with one sentence - "let's have lunch if you have offered your prayers" and that's when I would feel again - I belong to this place. 

## So what ? 

That's the part I realized very late in my life. I learned that all the great pleasures offered by some ultra luxury resorts or hotels failed to please me the way my stay in my place surrounded by people I love does.

And at this point, I think, Christopher McCandless (Alexander Supertramp) of *Into The Wild* fame was right on his conclusion which says - *Happiness is only real, when shared*. 

P.S. This doesn't mean I don't go on vacations.
